Backup models

The Model menu is your primary model: it does every step. If it fails on a step (no key, out of credit, service down or unreachable), the first backup answers that step instead, then the second, then the third. The run log says each time a backup steps in.

Blocks that name their own model, such as with model, keep that model, so a panel of judges stays a panel of different judges.
